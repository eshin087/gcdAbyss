# assets/models

- `hero-mixamo.glb`: the "Ninja" character and 24 clips of the "Great Sword Pack" from Adobe Mixamo
  (https://www.mixamo.com), converted for this game by `scripts/mixamo/convert.py` (ADR-294). The mask is removed,
  the textures are dropped, the body is printed in the game's letters.
- `hero-mixrig.glb`: the hero's own mesh, auto-rigged by Mixamo, with the same 24 clips.

Mixamo characters and animations are royalty-free for use in games and other projects; they may not be
redistributed as standalone assets. Only these converted game files are kept here, not the downloaded FBX files.
